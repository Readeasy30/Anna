## 📋 Section 1: The 4 Core Level 4 Android Job Descriptions

### 👤 1. Anna-WebsiteManager (Sales & Client Acquisition Core)
*   **Primary Objective:** Drive inbound leads through your public storefront website and close independent auto trade clients.
*   **Knowledge Base Requirements:**
    *   **Sales Psychology:** Mastery of consultative closing models, customer friction points, and value positioning.
    *   **Product Stack:** Full expertise in HTML5 UP trade skeletal frames, fast mobile layouts, and 73-hour rapid deployment parameters.
    *   **Customer Insight:** Deep understanding of local shop operations and regional market traffic trends.

### 👤 2. Maximus-Scraper (Market & Territory Intelligence)
*   **Primary Objective:** Scrape, parse, and catalog every high-value independent trade shop in a target city on auto-pilot.
*   **Knowledge Base Requirements:**
    *   **Directory Parsing:** Automated extraction methods for maps, business directories, and localized data sets.
    *   **Geographic Scaling:** Full mapping strings for Missouri test regions (Springfield, Branson, Hollister, Ridgedale) scaling into a nationwide A-Z zip code master database.
    *   **Lead Grading:** Rule matrices to instantly flag shops with broken, slow, or non-mobile-friendly websites.

### 👤 3. Sterling-CFO (Financial Ledger & Payout Security)
*   **Primary Objective:** Audit all subscription revenue pipelines, secure your accounts, and maintain clean cash flow tracking.
*   **Knowledge Base Requirements:**
    *   **Gateway Ledger:** Real-time monitoring of Stripe API webhooks, checkout tokens, and secure multi-step authentication access.
    *   **Subscription Architecture:** Financial logic tracking for recurring loops locked at our **$1,000/mo baseline rate**.
    *   **Zero-Inventory Models:** Digital ledger systems optimized for zero physical storage overhead operations.

### 👤 4. Samantha-Compliance (Ecosystem Shield & Legal Watchdog)
*   **Primary Objective:** Protect the corporate shield by vetting every single line of ad text and deployment layout code.
*   **Knowledge Base Requirements:**
    *   **Minor Protection:** Hardcoded boundaries preventing any promotional materials from targeting minors.
    *   **Transparency Terms:** Automatic injection of clear promotional disclosures into all frontend web footers.
    *   **Exemption Protocols:** Real-time tracking of state-by-state laws ensuring your referral engine operates strictly within license-free regulations.
